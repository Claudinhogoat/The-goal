Who Is Tami?

Tami is the kind of person who walks into a room and makes her presence felt. She has opinions, she has convictions, and she's not afraid to voice them. In a world full of people who stay quiet, Tami speaks up.

She knows what she believes in and she stands by it. That kind of conviction takes courage — and Tami has it in abundance.

Strong Beliefs, Stronger Voice

Tami doesn't do wishy-washy. When she believes in something, she believes in it with her whole heart. She'll debate you, she'll challenge you, and she'll make you think about things from angles you never considered.

"Well-behaved women seldom make history."

Tami's strong beliefs aren't just talk. She backs them up with action, research, and an unshakeable sense of what's right.
